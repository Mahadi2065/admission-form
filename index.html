Welcome to the CSE Department, Northern University Bangladesh

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Northern University Bangladesh is dedicated to providing a high-quality education in the field of computer science. We prepare our students for successful careers in software development, system administration, network engineering, and other related areas.

We offer a range of undergraduate and graduate programs designed to meet the evolving needs of the tech industry. Our curriculum is regularly updated to incorporate the latest technologies and best practices.

Admission Requirements

Undergraduate Programs

  • HSC or equivalent with a minimum GPA of 2.5
  • Mathematics with a minimum grade of C
  • Physics with a minimum grade of C
  • English with a minimum grade of C
  • Satisfactory score in the NUB admission test

Graduate Programs

  • Bachelor's degree in CSE or a related field
  • Minimum CGPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale
  • Satisfactory score in the NUB admission test or GRE
  • For some programs, relevant work experience may be required

How to Apply

  1. Visit the NUB website: www.nub.ac.bd
  2. Click on the "Admissions" tab.
  3. Select the program you want to apply for.
  4. Fill out the online application form.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the required documents.
  7. Prepare for the admission test.
